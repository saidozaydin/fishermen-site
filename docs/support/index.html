Support

Help for Fishermen: Pixel Fishing Game

Need help or found a bug? Email ozaydinsaidcp@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, Android version and the game version (shown on the Google Play page), and a screenshot if you can. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Fishermen has no accounts and works offline. Your progress is saved automatically on your phone.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. The game has no ads and nothing to buy. All coins and items are earned by fishing.

I changed phones. Can I keep my progress?

Progress is stored only on your device. If Android's device backup (for example "Backup by Google One") is turned on, Android may restore the game's progress when you set up a new phone with the same Google account. We cannot move or restore progress ourselves, because we never receive it.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Open the in-game menu, go to Settings and switch Notifications on or off. You can also manage them in Android Settings → Apps → Fishermen → Notifications.

How do I turn off vibration or the music?

In the in-game Settings you can switch off Vibration and adjust or mute music and sound effects.

How do I reset my progress or delete my data?

Open Android Settings → Apps → Fishermen → Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data), or uninstall the game. This permanently deletes your progress and settings. The game has no account, so there is nothing else to delete on our side. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The time of day or weather in the game looks wrong.

The in-game time and weather follow your phone's clock and are the same for every player. If they seem off, check that your phone's date, time and time zone are set automatically.

The game feels slow or stutters.

Close other apps running in the background and restart the game. If it still happens, email us your phone model so we can look into it.
