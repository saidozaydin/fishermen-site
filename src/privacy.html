Privacy Policy

Fishermen: Pixel Fishing Game · Effective 22 November 2026

The short version: Fishermen does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your device, and we run no servers that receive anything from the game.

This policy explains how the Android game Fishermen: Pixel Fishing Game (package com.saoz.fishermen, "the game") handles information. The game is made by SaOz ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, write to ozaydinsaidcp@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any other personal details.

The game does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not use advertising IDs.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your progress between sessions, the game saves a small file in its private storage on your phone. It contains only game data, for example:

  • coins, fish caught, record sizes, trophies and Career stars;
  • items you own and have equipped in the Market;
  • daily gift, quest and challenge progress;
  • your settings (music, sound, vibration, fish alerts, notifications) and whether the tutorial is finished;
  • which reminder notifications are scheduled and when one was last shown, so the game does not repeat them too often.

This file never leaves your device through the game. Only you and the game can read it. Other apps cannot access it.

Android backup: if you have turned on Android's own device backup (for example "Backup by Google One"), Android may include the game's save file in that backup so your progress can be restored on a new phone. This backup is controlled by you and by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot see it.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy the game uses it
NotificationsTo show optional local reminders, for example when a storm brings rare fish to your spot or a challenge is about to reset. They are planned on your phone; no notification server is involved. You can turn them off in the game's Settings (Notifications) or in Android's settings at any time.
VibrationFor short haptic feedback during fishing. You can turn it off in the game's Settings (Vibration).

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

4. Sharing a catch

After a special catch you can tap Share. The game then creates a picture of your catch, stores it in its private storage and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app (for example a messenger) and send it yourself. What happens after that is governed by the privacy policy of the app you choose.

5. Google Play services

At a happy moment the game may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating dialog. Google Play decides whether to show it, and the game is never told whether or how you rated it. Downloading the game and rating it on Google Play are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Google may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as install counts or crash rates) through the Google Play Console; these do not identify you.

6. Time and weather in the game

The in-game time of day and weather are calculated from your device's clock. The game does not use your location and does not contact a weather service.

7. Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we also do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device, so you are in full control of it. To delete it:

  • open Android Settings → Apps → Fishermen → Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data); or
  • uninstall the game.

Either way your progress is removed permanently and cannot be recovered by us. If you also want to remove a copy from your Android device backup, manage it in your Google account's backup settings.

If you emailed us, you can ask us to delete that conversation at any time.

9. Contacting us

If you write to us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you, do not share it with anyone and delete it once it is no longer needed.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example if we ever add advertising), we will update this page and the date above before that version of the game is released, and describe the change in the Google Play release notes.
